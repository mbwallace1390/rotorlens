RotorLens was founded and originally created in 2026 by **Michael Wallace**
([@mbwallace1390](https://github.com/mbwallace1390)). He established the
project's product direction and serves as its lead maintainer.

## Contributors

Additional human contributors are identified by the repository's Git history
and GitHub contributor records. Contributors retain copyright in their work
unless they make a separate written agreement, and contributions are accepted
under the terms described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## AI-assisted development

Some research, drafting, implementation, testing, and review has used
AI-assisted tools under human direction. Historical Git metadata includes
commits whose technical `Author` or `Committer` fields, `Co-Authored-By`
trailers, or session metadata name Claude or another AI-assisted tool. Those
records are retained as provenance of the development workflow; they do not
make an AI system a legal or copyright author, contributor, signatory, or
copyright holder.

For governance and licensing, the human maintainer who accepts and distributes
the resulting work is responsible for its provenance, licensing, safety, and
correctness. Material AI assistance should be disclosed in the relevant pull
request so reviewers can apply the appropriate provenance and verification
scrutiny.
