## Purpose and status

The RotorLens name and the project's logos, icons, and other distinctive
branding (together, the "RotorLens Marks") identify the project founded and
originally created by Michael Wallace and distinguish official RotorLens
releases from modified distributions.

No representation is made that a comprehensive trademark clearance search has
been completed, that any RotorLens Mark is registered, or that a claimed right
is valid in every jurisdiction. Do not use the registered trademark symbol
(`®`) for a RotorLens Mark unless a registration is actually granted in the
relevant jurisdiction. This policy is not a substitute for legal advice.

The Mozilla Public License 2.0 permits use, modification, and distribution of
covered source code subject to its terms. It does not grant rights to use the
RotorLens Marks. Nothing in this policy grants ownership of, or a license to
use, any mark except for the limited factual references described below.

## Factual references that do not need separate permission

You may make truthful, non-misleading statements about the project, including:

- linking to the official RotorLens repository;
- stating that software is compatible with RotorLens, when that statement is
  accurate; and
- stating **"Based on RotorLens, originally created by Michael Wallace"** for a
  modified distribution, provided the distribution is clearly identified as
  unofficial and the statement does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

Use only as much of the word mark as is reasonably needed for the factual
reference. Do not use a RotorLens logo or icon for a modified distribution
without written permission.

## Modified distributions and forks

A modified distribution must not create a reasonable impression that it is an
official RotorLens release. Unless written permission is granted, use a
distinct product name, application icon, package or bundle identifier, website,
and store listing. Prominently identify that the distribution is modified and
unofficial, identify its publisher, and preserve all copyright and license
notices required by the applicable source-code licenses.

Fork repository names may describe their relationship factually, but their
repository description and release materials must make the fork's unofficial
status clear. Domain names, organization names, social-media handles, and app
store publisher names must not imply that a fork is operated by or endorsed by
the official project.

## Uses requiring written permission

Written permission is required to:

- use a RotorLens Mark as the name or primary branding of a modified product,
  service, organization, event, domain, or publication;
- use a RotorLens logo or icon in a modified application or commercial
  offering;
- claim that a release, fork, service, or organization is official, certified,
  sponsored, or endorsed; or
- manufacture merchandise primarily branded with a RotorLens Mark.

Permission requests may be sent to
[mbwallace1390@gmail.com](mailto:mbwallace1390@gmail.com) with the subject
`RotorLens trademark request`. Do not include confidential information. A lack
of response is not permission.

## Reporting confusing use

Report potentially confusing use through the official repository. Do not post
private flight logs, configuration dumps, location data, device identifiers, or
other sensitive material in that report.
